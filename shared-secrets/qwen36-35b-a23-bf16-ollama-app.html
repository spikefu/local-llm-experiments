Shared Secrets

End-to-end encrypted communication between two parties.
All cryptography runs locally in your browser.

How it works:
  1. Click Generate Keypair below
  2. Copy your public key and share it with the person you want to communicate with
  3. Keep your private key secret — you’ll need it to decrypt their messages
Encrypt a message:
  1. Paste the sender’s public key above
  2. Type or paste your message below
  3. Click Encrypt and share the ciphertext
Decrypt a message:
  1. Import or paste your private key
  2. Paste the encrypted payload you received
  3. Click Decrypt to read the message
