End-to-End Encrypted

Secure Message Exchange

Generate cryptographic keys, encrypt messages with a recipient's public key, and decrypt them with your private key — all in your browser.

Generate New Key Pair

Creates a 2048-bit RSA key pair using your browser's built-in cryptographic engine. Your keys never leave this device.

The public key can be shared openly. The private key must be kept secret — anyone with it can decrypt messages meant for you.
✓ RSA-2048 ✓ OAEP-SHA256 ✓ AES-256-GCM
Keep your private key secure. Anyone who obtains this key can decrypt messages addressed to you. Store it safely and never share it.
Encrypt a Message

Paste the recipient's public key and your plaintext message. The output is a ciphertext packet containing an encrypted symmetric key and the encrypted message.

1

Get the recipient's public key

Ask the other party to generate keys (tab above) and share their public key with you.

2

Paste the public key below

Enter the full base64-encoded public key string.

3

Enter your message

Type or paste the plaintext message you want to encrypt.

4

Encrypt and share

Click Encrypt, then share the resulting ciphertext with the recipient.

Decrypt a Message

Paste the ciphertext you received and your own private key to recover the original message.

1

Receive the ciphertext

The other party will send you the encrypted ciphertext string.

2

Paste both ciphertext and your private key

Enter the ciphertext and your private key in the fields below.

3

Decrypt and read

Click Decrypt to recover the original plaintext message.

All cryptographic operations are performed locally in your browser using the Web Crypto API.
No data is ever transmitted over a network. Your keys and messages never leave this page.
