Secure Message Exchange

End-to-end encrypted communication — everything runs locally in your browser

⚠️
How it works: Each party generates their own RSA key pair locally. Public keys are shared openly. Messages encrypted with a recipient's public key can only be decrypted by the holder of the corresponding private key. No data ever leaves your browser.

📋 Communication Flow

1 You generate a key pair in this app (Step 1)
2 Copy your public key and send it to the other party
3 Paste the other party's public key into Step 2
4 Type your secret message and click Encrypt
5 Copy the encrypted output and send it to the other party
6 The other party pastes it into Step 3 and decrypts with their private key
